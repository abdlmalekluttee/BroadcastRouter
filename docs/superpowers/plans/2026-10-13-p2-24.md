## Pre-flight rulings

- Default continuous-video/optional-audio is deliberately fail-closed for legacy audio-only sources: they require an explicit AudioOnly profile before their next start. Existing healthy owners are not restarted to migrate. Cost: a legitimate undeclared audio-only input waits on standby. This is safer than silently converting missing video into permanent generated black.
- Probe proof and actual FFmpeg startup are separate sessions; qualification reduces, but cannot eliminate, the between-session track race. No claim of seamless graph insertion or historical root-cause certainty.
- Sparse input-video cadence is not a continuous-health requirement. It stays observable as Unknown after gaps and cannot vote for restart; output and required audio retain existing thresholds. Cost: sparse video-only decoder stalls lack two independent failed signals and cannot be automatically distinguished from intentional stills.
- Existing private evidence/ledger is retained; no cleanup of user evidence. Task-specific report supersedes generic full-review artifact requirements.
