Status: bounded implementation locally verified, **not deployed**. Branch
`fix/P2-24-probe-media-policy`, based on P2-21 `b288a12`. Only the owner merges.

## Scope and established causes

A short probe inferred audio-led mode from a low frame count, discarded legitimate
sparse pictures, and could label metadata/packet-only media ready. The selected
FFmpeg graph then mapped generated black. Later metadata cannot alter that graph.

The owner approved the bounded solution: declared expectations, qualification
before replacing standby, and no healthy-owner restart for reclassification.
**Seamless insertion of a truly new track remains OPEN.** Local custom8.1.2 and
[upstream FFmpeg](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/master/fftools/ffmpeg_demux.c)
discard streams introduced outside the initialized input set. Optional mapping
ignores an absent match, not binds future tracks. A newer compilation alone is
not a demonstrated fix. No binary/analysis flags changed. A persistent output
writer with a separate ingest/switch boundary needs its own design/qualification.

Historical evidence establishes a publisher edge, mandatory video-map failure
and later successful attempt. Without retained SDP/packet/analysis evidence,
late track declaration versus insufficient analysis cannot be distinguished.
Fresh decoded-track proof reduces risk but a separate probe session cannot
eliminate the between-session track race. Do not claim that history resolved.

## Configuration and migration

`SourceMediaProfiles` is saved in existing transactional SQLite settings and
preserved by UI drafts. Each entry uses the exact canonical source identity:

```json
{"SourceMediaProfiles":[
  {"SourceId":"TEST/live/_definst_/synthetic","Expectation":1,"RequireAudio":true}
]}
```

Expectation:0=ContinuousVideo,1=SparseVideo,2=AudioOnly. No new GUI controls.
Duplicate/invalid identities and unsupported modes are rejected before writes.
Absent entry means **ContinuousVideo with optional audio**, not inferred AudioOnly.
Audio may be absent when optional, but advertised/selected audio must decode PCM.
AudioOnly always requires PCM and deliberately selects black; required audio
cannot qualify a muted preset. Exact identity renames require updating the key.

Legacy audio-only inputs require an explicit declaration before their next start.
Existing healthy owners are not restarted to migrate. No named-stream exceptions.
Continuous/sparse video both require a decoded frame, not a cadence threshold.
Advertised video without a frame is WaitingForKeyframe; absent required track,
invalid schema or missing PCM is Unknown. Previous metadata is not fresh proof.
Declaration/config/publisher generations bind the2s single-use recovery proof.
The same proof boundary applies to initial and manual starts. A pending initial
start remains WaitingForStream and keeps port standby; normal reconciliation
revisits it. Unknown/WaitingForKeyframe observations clear old absence timers and
cannot retire a running fallback owner. Explicit operator disable remains valid.

Sparse input gaps become diagnostic Unknown, not Failed, with last advancement
visible. Output/PCM thresholds stay1s suspect+3s confirmation,20s startup,2s
recovery; corroborated failures still recover. Sparse video-only decoder death
cannot be distinguished from intentional stills by two independent signals.
Mode observations are consumed once and never schedule restart. Snapshots retain
immutable `SelectedMedia` separately from source metadata; logs describe the
**next start**, not a fictional switch in the running output graph.
Published snapshots and the Sources media cell separately show observed
qualification and the current session's selected graph. Decision evidence carries
the selected expectation/video flag without raw operational identifiers.
