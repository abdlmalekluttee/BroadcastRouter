## Established defect and scoped change

The audio decoder and empty-audio-queue classifiers used warning counts as a
substitute for elapsed failure. Multiple messages from one packet, or queue
warnings one millisecond apart, could become a failure category. The supervisor
then kept the first category indefinitely through `??=`, even after recovery.

Classification now requires independently measured InputAudio to be Failed,
with a known last advancement at least four seconds old. This reuses P2-22's
monotonic media model, not warning multiplicity or audio amplitude. Advancing
digital silence remains healthy. Missing, invalid or generated input evidence
does not prove failed input PCM. Raw stderr and anomaly history are retained.

The supervisor supplies current media evidence to classifiers. It keeps the
latest classified observation and clears a current fault only after the relevant
media has reached the existing two-second Healthy qualification. Unknown does
not clear faults. Both snapshot paths compare-and-clear the exact observed fault;
a newer diagnostic racing the snapshot survives. A category is still context,
not permission to restart: the existing two-independent-signal coordinator gate
remains authoritative. Audio resampling and output commands are unchanged.

- Stderr has no reliable packet identity. Removing count-based authorization
  makes repeated diagnostics idempotent policy inputs; do not invent packet IDs.
  Cost: instrumentation is required to prove audio failure.

  Thresholds remain provisional until measured GOP/jitter bench qualification.
- Video warning/duplicate policy belongs to P2-21; track expectations to P2-24.
  Cost: intermediate branches are intentionally not deployment candidates.
- Unknown instrumentation remains a deliberate safety tradeoff: it cannot prove
  failure or recovery. It is not a claim that an uninstrumented decoder is healthy.
- The reviewer did not run the suite, build, privacy/provenance or hosted jobs.
  These require separately recorded author verification, not inferred review credit.
- Gate C and deployment remain OPEN until the entire matrix and Gate B hardware
