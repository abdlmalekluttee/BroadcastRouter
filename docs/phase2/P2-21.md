duplicates still produce diagnostic suspicion after the configured elapsed window,

with the unchanged R05 correctness probe. Queue warnings require InputVideo Failed
with a known advancement at least four seconds old. Audio or output-only evidence
does not substitute. Healthy/Unknown video does not classify as failed.

no new output arguments, formats, operator controls, thresholds, service actions
